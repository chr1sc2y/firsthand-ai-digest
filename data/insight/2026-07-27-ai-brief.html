LLM Token Relay Market and Fraud

Investigation Reveals Thriving Black Market for Discounted LLM Tokens

HIGH

Facts

  • Matt Lenhard's investigation details a market, primarily in China, where resellers offer discounted LLM tokens via proxies. S001
  • Discounts are achieved by abusing free trials, proxying through unprotected support bots, and using stolen credit cards or chargeback attacks. S001
  • The proxies use open-source software: one-api and its fork new-api, which are legitimate API proxy products. S001
  • Buyers seek cheap tokens, circumvent geo-restrictions, and sometimes collect data for model distillation. S001

Analysis. This ecosystem exploits lax API key management and the absence of strict spending caps. It represents a systemic risk for LLM providers and legitimate users who may face higher costs or service degradation.

Implication. LLM vendors must urgently implement granular spending limits and anomaly detection to curb abuse. Developers should secure endpoints and avoid exposing raw API keys.

Uncertainty. The scale of the market and total revenue lost by providers are not quantified in the source.
