HIGH

Facts

Asana Completes 5-Year Engineering Project in 2 Weeks with Codex

HIGH

Facts

  • Asana used OpenAI Codex to remove an outdated testing system (Enzyme) in about two weeks, a task previously estimated to take five years. S006

Implication. Software development teams can tackle previously infeasible projects, but human oversight remains crucial to ensure quality and correctness.

Uncertainty. The generalizability of this success to other projects and codebases is uncertain; not all tasks may be as amenable to automation.
