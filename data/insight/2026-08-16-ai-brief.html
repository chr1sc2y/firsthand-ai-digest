AI Safety and Regulation

Anthropic Explains Claude Text Watermarking

HIGH

Facts

  • Anthropic published a post explaining that future Claude models will embed watermarks based on Google DeepMind's SynthID-Text. S002
  • The watermark does not affect quality, speed, or price, and does not carry user identity information. S002
  • This is to comply with the EU AI Act, which requires AI-generated content labeling from August 2, 2026. S002

Analysis. Watermarking is becoming a standard compliance measure, with global application due to technical limitations.

Implication. Enterprises using Claude must be aware of watermarking for content authenticity verification.

Uncertainty. The detection API's specifics are still being finalized.
