HIGH

Facts

  • Researcher Håkon Måløy demonstrated a prompt injection attack against Microsoft Word Copilot that can self-replicate by copying hidden instructions into new documents. S002
  • The attack was responsibly disclosed to Microsoft, who had 144 days to work on a fix, but no mitigation covering the full class of attack exists. S002

Analysis. This represents an escalation from static prompt injection to a worm-like propagation mechanism, raising the stakes for enterprise AI deployment where documents are frequently reused.

Implication. Organizations using Copilot for Word should treat all external documents as untrusted and implement strict content sanitization until Microsoft releases a comprehensive fix.

Uncertainty. The real-world prevalence and ease of exploitation outside controlled demonstrations are not yet known.

MCP Specification Update to Stateless Protocol

HIGH

Facts

  • The Model Context Protocol (MCP) released specification 2026-07-28, shifting from a bidirectional stateful model to a stateless request-response model, removing session IDs. S007
  • The update introduces multi-round requests, header-based routing, and aligns authorization with OAuth 2.0; SDKs for TypeScript, Python, Go, and C# have been updated. S007

Analysis. The move to statelessness simplifies load balancing and scalability for MCP servers, potentially accelerating adoption in production environments.
