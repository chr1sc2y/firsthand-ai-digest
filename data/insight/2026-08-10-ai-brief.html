Anthropic Makes Auto Mode Default in Claude Code

HIGH

Facts

  • Anthropic is making auto mode the default setting for new sessions in Claude Code for Pro, Max, and Team plans starting on August 14th. S002
  • In a controlled study with 1,053 paid testers, only 13.6% of humans refused a clearly dangerous command, while auto mode would have blocked 89% of those actions. S002
