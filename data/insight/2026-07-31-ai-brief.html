HIGH

Facts

  • Enabling 'retain reasoning' and 'compression' API settings tripled GPT-5.6 Sol's ARC-AGI-3 score from 13.3% to 38.3% with 6x fewer output tokens. S004 S058
  • François Chollet confirmed that general-purpose API settings not developed for ARC-AGI-3 are acceptable for benchmarking. S043

Analysis. The dramatic improvement highlights how benchmark results can be sensitive to inference configuration. OpenAI's discovery that the harness was discarding reasoning context suggests that many models may be underperforming on benchmarks due to suboptimal settings.

Implication. API developers should experiment with these settings to maximize model performance on complex reasoning tasks.

Uncertainty. The generalizability of these settings to other benchmarks and real-world tasks is not yet established.
