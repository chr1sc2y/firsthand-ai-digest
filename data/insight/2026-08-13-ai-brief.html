Security and Vulnerabilities

Researchers steal hidden reasoning traces from proprietary LLM APIs

HIGH

Facts

  • Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google return encrypted chain-of-thought blocks that can be replayed across sessions, users, and models. S007
  • Researchers injected a strong model's encrypted reasoning into a weaker sibling, jailbroke it, and recovered the stronger model's hidden reasoning in plaintext. S007 S004
  • All model providers acknowledged the report and subsequently fixed the vulnerability, preventing the same attacks. S007

Analysis. Analysis: This vulnerability highlights the fragility of encrypted reasoning and the potential for cross-model attacks, though it has been patched.

Implication. AI providers must strengthen encryption key management and monitor for replay attacks.

Uncertainty. The full extent of the attack's real-world exploitation is unknown.
