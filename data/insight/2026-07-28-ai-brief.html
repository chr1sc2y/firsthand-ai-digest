OpenAI Research: AI Expands Job Boundaries

HIGH

Facts

  • Analysis of 800,000 ChatGPT messages shows 16.8% of work-related messages and 43.5% of occupation-specific messages are about tasks associated with another occupation. S003
  • Task crossover is highest in customer experience (77%), design (75%), HR (69%), legal (56%), and marketing (53%). S003
  • Small businesses show more task crossover: 18.9% for 2-5 seat workspaces vs. 16.3% for over 100 seats. S003

Analysis. AI is enabling workers to perform tasks outside their traditional roles, effectively flattening organizational hierarchies and reducing handoffs. This could lead to more generalist roles and smaller teams.

Implication. Enterprises should redesign job descriptions and training to account for expanded role boundaries, and small businesses can leverage AI to cover multiple functions without hiring specialists.

Uncertainty. The study is based on ChatGPT users only and may not generalize to other AI tools or non-users.
